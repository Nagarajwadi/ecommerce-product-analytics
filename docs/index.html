Key Finding

The largest drop-off occurs between viewing a product and adding it to the cart. A potential next step is to investigate product page engagement, pricing, and add-to-cart usability.

Note: Results are based on a synthetic dataset created for this project.

Project Overview

An end-to-end product analytics project exploring customer journeys, product engagement, shopping cart behavior, and purchase conversion. The project uses synthetic e-commerce event data to identify funnel drop-offs and demonstrate data-driven product analysis.

Key Insights

  • 97.9% of users viewed a product after opening the app.
  • 31.28% dropped off between product viewing and adding to cart.
  • 29.50% dropped off between adding to cart and starting checkout.
  • 79.19% of checkout starters reached purchase.

These results highlight opportunities to improve product discovery, cart engagement, and the checkout experience.
