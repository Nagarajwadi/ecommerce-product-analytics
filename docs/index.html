Project Overview

An end-to-end product analytics project exploring customer journeys, product engagement, shopping cart behavior, and purchase conversion. The project uses synthetic e-commerce event data to identify funnel drop-offs and demonstrate data-driven product analysis.

Key Insights

  • 97.9% of users viewed a product after opening the app.
  • 31.28% dropped off between product viewing and adding to cart.
  • 29.50% dropped off between adding to cart and starting checkout.
  • 79.19% of checkout starters reached purchase.

These results highlight opportunities to improve product discovery, cart engagement, and the checkout experience.

Business Recommendations

Funnel analysis helps identify where to investigate friction in the customer journey. The following recommendations are hypotheses to validate with further analysis and experiments.

1. Improve Product-to-Cart Conversion

Finding: 3,062 users dropped off between viewing a product and adding it to the cart.

Recommendation: Investigate product-page clarity, pricing, delivery information, and Add to Cart usability.

Metric: Product-view-to-cart conversion rate.

2. Reduce Checkout Abandonment

Finding: 987 users dropped off between starting checkout and completing a purchase.

Recommendation: Investigate unexpected fees, payment failures, and checkout form complexity.

Metric: Checkout-to-purchase conversion rate.

3. Validate Changes with A/B Testing

Recommendation: Test a product-page improvement against the existing experience before rolling it out.

Primary metric: Add-to-cart conversion rate. Guardrail: Purchase conversion rate.

These recommendations are hypotheses based on a synthetic dataset. Their impact must be validated through further analysis or controlled experiments.
